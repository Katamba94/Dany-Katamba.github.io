About Me

I am a specialized Research Engineer and Data Scientist focusing on Structural Health Monitoring (SHM), Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), and Prognostics and Health Management (PHM).

Currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Politecnico di Bari, I combine signal processing, machine learning, and deep learning (CNN, Autoencoders) to solve complex engineering challenges in aerospace materials and rotating machinery. My work bridges the gap between experimental mechanics and advanced AI diagnostics.

Current Research Projects

I-MOBI Project (2025 - Present)

R&D on design and testing methodologies for vehicle components. Focus on energy efficiency, CO2 reduction, and lightweight structures using mechanical characterization and FEM simulations.

TUBES Project (2023 - 2024)

Application of advanced Acoustic Emission (AE) methods for hazard assessment in lava tube structures. Developed AI-based predictive models for structural evaluation under dynamic conditions.

RIPARTI Project (2023 - 2025)

Development of intelligent monitoring systems for industrial customized vehicles. Implementation of vibration-based fault diagnosis in rotating joints using CNN architectures and kurtogram-based spectral analysis.
